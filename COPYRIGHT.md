This repository hosts non‑free third‑party materials (e.g. third‑party logos, movie posters) for use by this organization.

All rights in the original works remain with their respective owners. We make limited use of these materials under applicable fair use, fair dealing, or other copyright exceptions in relevant jurisdictions.

### Reason:

MontageSubs is a non‑profit fan subtitle community. We respect the rights of creators, but occasionally need to host third‑party materials for criticism, comment, reporting, or scholarly purposes in order to convey meaning accurately. Such hosting is therefore limited and purposeful.

If you are a rights holder and believe material has been included improperly, please open a GitHub issue.

Principles we follow:
- Prefer free/licensed alternatives whenever available.  
- When non‑free material is necessary, use it only minimally (reduced resolution, limited scope).  
- Document the purpose for inclusion.  
- Honor valid takedown requests from rights holders.
